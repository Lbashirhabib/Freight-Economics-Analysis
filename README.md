# Team 5: Freight Economics and Customer Geography

    **Session:** Saturday 31 October 2026, 6:00 pm UK time  
    **Main room:** https://meet.google.com/rqg-sity-yvx  
    **Build time:** 40 minutes  
    **Presentation:** 5 minutes, exactly two presenters

    ## Business question

    Where is freight cost putting the greatest pressure on order economics?

    ## Data assets

    - `data/olist_orders_dataset.csv`
- `data/olist_order_items_dataset.csv`
- `data/olist_customers_dataset.csv`

    ## Build brief

    1. Aggregate item price and freight to one order before comparing regions.
2. Calculate freight per order and freight-to-item-value ratio.
3. Compare customer states and flag high-cost, meaningful-volume segments.
4. Build one chart and recommend one testable logistics action.

    ## Minimum deliverables

    - A working notebook or SQL-backed analysis
    - A written grain statement and at least one validated join
    - Three to five useful metrics
    - One decision-focused chart
    - Three findings, one limitation and one recommended next action
    - A five-minute presentation delivered by exactly two team members

    ## 40-minute sprint

    - Minutes 0-5: assign roles and choose the two presenters
    - Minutes 5-10: agree the grain, questions and success measures
    - Minutes 10-25: build the analysis
    - Minutes 25-32: validate joins and metrics
    - Minutes 32-37: finish the chart and story
    - Minutes 37-40: rehearse

    ## Responsible AI rule

    AI is allowed for syntax suggestions, debugging, explaining errors and chart formatting. The team must validate joins and metrics, understand every line, and briefly disclose where AI helped. Blind copy-and-paste and invented findings are not acceptable.

    ## Suggested roles

    Facilitator/timekeeper, data lead, quality checker and storyteller. Everyone contributes, even when one person is sharing their screen.
